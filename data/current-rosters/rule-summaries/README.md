[Rule summaries](../../../docs/rule-summaries.md) defines the records and [catalogue releases](../../../docs/catalogue-releases.md) explains how to build them.

Every summary is original wording, checked against the official sources it cites. They are pre-release drafts that no person has reviewed yet.

Every record cites the signed app build in force at the update's cutoff, Warhammer 40,000: The App 2.7.1, data build 972, released 2026-10-02. 599 records cite the Games Workshop PDF page that prints the rule, which is then their `source`. 19 records rest on a reading the sources leave open and carry an `interpretation` flag.

Typed facts follow the [rule facts](../../../docs/rule-facts.md) format: 818 Enhancements carry their eligibility and 197 awards their scoring from the app build's rows, and drafted structure is in `detail`.

The `rosters`, `profiles` and `points` categories hold the update's datasheets, built from the app build: 1,056 units with roster definitions (1,311 wargear options), 1,116 stat lines and 5,172 weapon profiles, and the `current-rosters` points snapshot of 1,055 units, 252 Detachments and 818 Enhancements. Datasheets are modelled once, not once per Space Marine chapter; each unit lists the BSData entry IDs it stands for as `aliases`. 307 Warhammer Legends datasheets the app does not carry come from Games Workshop's Legends PDFs, with their profiles and, in the `current-rosters-legends` snapshot, the costs of 297 of them from the Munitorum Field Manual's Legends section. 291 Legends datasheets have roster definitions typed from the PDF's composition, equipment and wargear options (30 of them with options left out, listed below), and their weapon profiles name that wargear.
